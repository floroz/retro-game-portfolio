# Adventure font: Pixel Operator

Chosen on 2026-10-05 after previewing **Pixel Operator**, **Pixelify Sans**,
**m6x11** (m6x11plus), and **VT323** on the About artwork, toolbar, and dialogue.

Pixel Operator gave us the best balance of readable text and the LucasArts
adventure feel of _Sam & Max Hit the Road_ and _Full Throttle_. Pixelify Sans
was wider and more playful; m6x11 heavier and denser; VT323 felt more like a
terminal. These are inspired alternatives, not the games' original fonts.

Apply it to desktop adventure text; retain the Windows 98 shell and Pocket
Adventure typography. The local font is by Jayvee Enaguas, licensed CC0 (see
`src/assets/fonts/PixelOperator-LICENSE.txt`).

The runnable comparison is preserved on branch
[`floroz/prototype-font-comparison`](https://github.com/floroz/retro-game-portfolio/tree/floroz/prototype-font-comparison)
at `/?font-preview&variant=operator` under `npm run dev`.
