# London jukebox

Built-in image generation, 30 September 2026. Transparent single-object sprite.

A vintage pub jukebox, front view with a little right side visible, viewed slightly
from above. Classic arched walnut cabinet with brass trim and amber/turquoise
illuminated glass tubes. Visible vinyl mechanism above cream song-selection
cards (abstract lines, no readable text), dark speaker grille below with a brass
fan motif. Warm London pub lighting and crisp painted 1990s adventure-game style.
No people, scenery, logo, words, coins or detached parts. Full centered object,
clean transparent margins, no floor shadow. Approximately 2:3 proportions,
intended for 66x102 art pixels at density 2.

Preserve the existing London palette and hard alpha. Preparation is reproducible
with `node --import tsx scripts/assets/jukebox.ts`.
