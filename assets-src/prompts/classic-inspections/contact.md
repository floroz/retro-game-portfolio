ART DIRECTION: Sam & Max Hit the Road and Full Throttle. The approved reference controls the degree of stylisation: strong irregular dark ink contours, deliberately stepped raster edges, compact pixel clusters, matte surfaces, 2–4 hard-edged shades per material, dramatic navy/violet shadow masses, warm ochre light, selective restrained dithering. Wry hand-drawn adventure-game personality, slightly crooked perspective and characterful graphic silhouettes. Simplify ornate detail into a few well chosen shapes. The image should read as a carefully drawn low-resolution DOS adventure screen enlarged with nearest-neighbour, not a contemporary cartoon illustration with a filter. Maintain sunny/playful appeal, avoid grim horror mood.
Keep 2:1 wide framing. Preserve the existing concept and main objects, with freedom to adjust incidental shapes to feel more authored, humorous and period appropriate. No additional characters or unrelated objects.
CRITICAL LIVE-TEXT AREA: The existing large blank panel on the RIGHT is functional space for HTML text. Retain its placement and outer extent. Keep a simple pale cream, almost uniform reading surface with no inscriptions, internal decoration, stains, shadows or objects invading the specified safe rectangle. Any thick borders, bolts or supports must stay outside that rectangle. Use crisp shapes with no artificial sharpening halos.
AVOID glossy plastic, smooth high-resolution vector curves, soft airbrush, bloom, realistic reflections, 3D lighting, sparkling lemon pores, children's mobile-game aesthetic, candy highlights, perfect symmetry, excessive miniature architectural detail, fake CRT scanlines, labels or watermarks. Do not import the man, suitcase, notebook or Sorrento scene from image 2 into this unrelated scene. This is the SAME rendering style applied to input 1's concept.
